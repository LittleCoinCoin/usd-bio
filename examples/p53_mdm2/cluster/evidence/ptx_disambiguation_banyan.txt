--- INTERPRETATION ---
cuobjdump -lptx names each record with the .ptx extension and an sm_NN token;
cuobjdump -lelf names each record with the .cubin extension and an sm_NN token.
PTX_CUBIN_TOKENS=0 means NO cubin name appears in the -lptx listing at all, so the
sm_NN token in a PTX record is that PTX record's OWN target architecture and not the
name of an enclosing cubin. The per-arch record counts are equal and non-zero for both
architectures, so PTX is embedded for sm_70 AND sm_90.
CONSEQUENCE: Success Gate 2's prediction ('-lptx shows sm_90 only, matching
GMX_CUDA_TARGET_COMPUTE=90') is FALSIFIED by observation, and the alternative
'enclosing-cubin naming' reading proposed in R14 is ALSO falsified. GROMACS' CMake
emits PTX for every GMX_CUDA_TARGET_SM entry regardless of GMX_CUDA_TARGET_COMPUTE.
This confirms the correction already recorded in Dockerfile and gromacs.def on
2026-07-30. PTX_COMPUTE_TOKENS=0 explains why no compute_* token was ever found:
cuobjdump names PTX records by sm_NN, so sass_audit.sh's compute_[0-9]+ alternative
cannot match. Functionally harmless: more PTX means more forward-portability at the
cost of a marginally larger binary. The build flag is UNCHANGED.
